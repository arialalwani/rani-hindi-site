About your teacher

Rani Sekhri

Rani has been teaching Hindi for 25 years, working one-on-one with foreigners and non-Hindi-speaking Indians who want to actually speak the language — not just study it. Classes run online or in person from Bandra West and are built around real conversation: the everyday Hindi you need for markets, taxis, family and friends.
